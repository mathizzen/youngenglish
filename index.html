OUR STORY

How YEA started & how it's going

Founded by educator Gayan Vimukthi, Young English Academy began as one classroom in Kiribathgoda with a simple goal: break language barriers and build real, confident communication.

Today YEA is a growing multi-branch network. Our mission is to give every learner the self-belief to present themselves to the world with courage.
